## Scoring method and hard gates

Each editorial rating is 0–5: 0 missing, 1 poor, 2 weak, 3 partial, 4 strong, 5 exceptional. Weighted total = Σ(weight × rating / 5). Apply the user's proposed thresholds: total ≥85, HLNI ≥4 and differentiation ≥4. These are reasoned editorial judgements, not an objective measurement of creativity.

## Cross-channel finding and next step

The plans select different reasoning tools, but the finished stories repeatedly use a student, brochures/cards, a desk and a notebook. Trust delivers a partial audit, Connection loses its planned peer exchange and mismatches the observation with its question, and Discovery ends with the Trust-like instruction to compare assessment criteria. Concept metadata and six-stage coverage do not prove that the intended distinctions survived into the screenplay.

Prioritise semantic fidelity between the selected concept and the actual story: observation → matching question → demonstrated consequence. Review the finished scripts for distinct channel experiences, then generate and score a separate run with the unchanged rubric. Do not advance to staging based on this run.
