# JOY UNIVERSITY — 3 Creative Reference Reels

Phase 3 · Creative-Quality Benchmark · Topic: B.Com vs BBA · English · 9:16

Creative standard: Every Reel must tell one connected story, deliver at least one High-Leverage New Information (HLNI) and end with a meaningful payoff.

These three references intentionally use different ideas, settings and storytelling devices.

TRUST — “The Syllabus Surprise”

Evidence-led investigation · 4 shots · 36 seconds

HLNI: Degree titles do not reliably identify which subjects are exclusive to either programme.

CONNECTION — “Try My Course for Five Minutes”

Two-friend mini experiment · 4 shots · 38 seconds

HLNI: Testing the kind of academic work you enjoy can reveal more than simply comparing course names.

DISCOVERY — “The Impossible Quiz”

Interactive reveal · 3 shots · 24 seconds

HLNI: Marketing can be a core subject in a B.Com programme, not just a BBA programme.

## 01. TRUST — The Syllabus Surprise

Story: A confident assumption is tested against actual university syllabi. The viewer learns a practical method to compare courses.

SHOT 01 — The Assumption

00:00–00:06

Hook

Problem

VISUAL ACTION

A presenter writes B.COM = ACCOUNTING and BBA = MANAGEMENT on a transparent board.

DIALOGUE / VOICEOVER

“B.Com is all about accounting. BBA is all about management. Right?”

CAMERA DIRECTION

Medium frontal shot. Focus shifts from the presenter to the two statements.

ON-SCREEN GRAPHIC

`IS THIS REALLY TRUE?`

SHOT 02 — The Evidence

00:06–00:16

Pattern Interrupt

Value

VISUAL ACTION

The presenter wipes away the statements. Actual University of Delhi B.Com and IGNOU BBA course structures appear side by side, with Financial Accounting highlighted in both.

DIALOGUE / VOICEOVER

“Here's the surprise. Financial Accounting appears in both of these university programmes. The subjects aren't completely separate.”

CAMERA DIRECTION

Overhead document close-ups. Ensure the official programme names are legible.

ON-SCREEN GRAPHIC

`FINANCIAL ACCOUNTING → BOTH`

SHOT 03 — What to Compare

00:16–00:29

Value

VISUAL ACTION

Three columns appear on a comparison worksheet: Subjects, Practical Work, Assessment. The presenter ticks each one.

DIALOGUE / VOICEOVER

“So check which subjects you'll study, what practical work you'll do, and how you'll be assessed. That's a much better comparison.”

CAMERA DIRECTION

Controlled overhead sequence. Each checklist item enters as it is spoken.

ON-SCREEN GRAPHIC

`SUBJECTS / PRACTICAL WORK / ASSESSMENT`

SHOT 04 — A Better Decision

00:29–00:36

Payoff

CTA

VISUAL ACTION

The two original labels are replaced by two completed comparison worksheets.

DIALOGUE / VOICEOVER

“Don't choose from the title. Choose from what you'll actually learn. Save this checklist.”

CAMERA DIRECTION

Return to the opening angle for visual closure.

ON-SCREEN GRAPHIC

`COMPARE BEFORE YOU CHOOSE`

Evidence: Official B.Com programme structure at the University of Delhi and BBA course structure at IGNOU.&#x20;

## 02. CONNECTION — Try My Course for Five Minutes

Story: Two friends disagree about which course is right for them. Instead of debating which degree is better, they try small examples of academic tasks.

SHOT 01 — The Disagreement

00:00–00:09

Hook

Problem

VISUAL ACTION

Two friends at a canteen table. One is viewing a B.Com course page; the other is viewing BBA.

DIALOGUE / VOICEOVER

Student A: “I'm choosing B.Com. You should too!”

Student B: “But I think I'd enjoy BBA more.”

Student A: “How do you know?”

CAMERA DIRECTION

Natural two-shot with quick reaction cuts.

ON-SCREEN GRAPHIC

English subtitles only.

SHOT 02 — The Challenge

00:09–00:17

Pattern Interrupt

Value

VISUAL ACTION

Student B closes both course pages and places two activity sheets on the table: a simple accounting exercise and a marketing campaign brief.

DIALOGUE / VOICEOVER

Student B: “Let's try something. One accounting task, one marketing challenge. Then we'll see what we enjoy.”

CAMERA DIRECTION

Close-up of the two sheets landing on the table.

ON-SCREEN GRAPHIC

`DON'T JUST COMPARE NAMES. TRY THE WORK.`

SHOT 03 — The Discovery

00:17–00:30

Value

VISUAL ACTION

Quick cuts of both students trying the tasks. Student A is absorbed in balancing figures; Student B starts sketching a campaign idea.

DIALOGUE / VOICEOVER

Student A: “Okay, I actually enjoyed solving that.”

Student B: “And I loved planning the campaign.”

Student A: “So maybe we should compare the actual assignments too.”

CAMERA DIRECTION

Natural handheld close-ups of hands, task sheets and reactions.

ON-SCREEN GRAPHIC

`WHAT KIND OF WORK DO YOU ENJOY?`

SHOT 04 — The Takeaway

00:30–00:38

Payoff

VISUAL ACTION

Both students keep their preferred worksheets. They open the course pages again, now looking for the relevant subjects and assignments.

DIALOGUE / VOICEOVER

Student B: “Different interests, different choices?”

Student A: “Maybe. Let's check the full syllabi first.”

CAMERA DIRECTION

Wide natural two-shot. End on the friends examining their options together.

ON-SCREEN GRAPHIC

`EXPLORE THE WORK. THEN CHOOSE.`

HLNI payoff: The viewer gains a practical self-assessment exercise. Trying a representative task is not a substitute for studying a full curriculum, but it can reveal which learning activities feel engaging. The Reel does not imply that marketing belongs only to BBA or accounting only to B.Com.

## 03. DISCOVERY — The Impossible Quiz

Story: A viewer is presented with what appears to be an easy course-identification quiz. The surprising answer exposes a common misconception.

SHOT 01 — Guess the Degree

00:00–00:06

Hook

Problem

VISUAL ACTION

An overhead tabletop shot shows a single card labelled PRINCIPLES OF MARKETING, positioned between two labelled boxes.

DIALOGUE / VOICEOVER

“Principles of Marketing. Which degree includes this subject? B.Com or BBA?”

CAMERA DIRECTION

Fixed overhead shot, high-contrast typography and an animated three-second countdown.

ON-SCREEN GRAPHIC

`B.COM?  BBA?` · `3...2...1`

SHOT 02 — The Unexpected Answer

00:06–00:16

Pattern Interrupt

Value

VISUAL ACTION

A hand places the card in the BBA box. A second identical card slides into the B.Com box. Actual university course lists appear, highlighting Principles of Marketing in both.

DIALOGUE / VOICEOVER

“Both! Marketing appears in these B.Com and BBA programmes. Surprised?”

CAMERA DIRECTION

Rapid physical-card reveal, followed by a clear two-second evidence hold.

ON-SCREEN GRAPHIC

`CORRECT ANSWER: BOTH`

SHOT 03 — The Real Answer

00:16–00:24

Payoff

VISUAL ACTION

More subject cards fill the boxes. The camera pulls back to reveal a wider range of subjects in both programmes.

DIALOGUE / VOICEOVER

“One subject doesn't define a degree. Check the complete syllabus before choosing.”

CAMERA DIRECTION

Smooth overhead pullback. End on an uncluttered two-column composition.

ON-SCREEN GRAPHIC

`ONE SUBJECT ≠ THE WHOLE COURSE`

Evidence: Principles of Marketing is listed in the University of Delhi B.Com programme and IGNOU BBA course structure.&#x20;
