## Editorial basis

- Trust: academic understanding, evidence, informed course/college decisions, and authentic expertise.
- Connection: supportive guidance, relatable student situations, confidence, family conversations, and belonging.
- Discovery: curiosity, participation, visual exploration, challenges, and broad +2 reach.

Channel is separate from tone and format. For example, a faculty Q&A remains Trust even if it originally appeared under Trendy; anxiety guidance becomes Connection even if originally Informative. Discovery does not claim that a sound or format is currently trending.

The original library contains a repeated title in two treatment groups; both entries are retained rather than silently deleted. No private saved projects are modified. These categories organize topics; they do not yet add a channel-specific voice to the generation request.
